                12 Days: the Grand Tour of Morocco from Marrakech

                Twelve unhurried days: Marrakech with a local guide, the kasbahs of the south, two nights in Merzouga and a camel trek, the cedar forests, Fes, Rabat and Casablanca.

                12Days
                11Nights: 10 riads + desert camp
                ~2,100Km in one full loop
                2Guided city days: Marrakech & Fes
                Overview

                This is our most complete journey from Marrakech: twelve days to see Morocco at a human pace, with time to stop, to walk and to stay a second night where it matters. It begins and ends at Marrakech airport, so you only have to land.

                After a full day in Marrakech with a local guide, from the souks of the medina to the Majorelle Garden, the road crosses the High Atlas to Ait Ben Haddou and Ouarzazate, then follows the Rose Valley and the Todra Gorge to the dunes of Merzouga.

                You spend two days in the Sahara. The first explores the area around Erg Chebbi, with Gnawa music in the village of Khamlia; the second is the camel trek itself, led by a local guide and the camp team, to a night in the dunes with Berber drums, a traditional dinner and a sky full of stars.

                The way north climbs the Ziz Valley to Midelt and through the cedar forests of Azrou, home to the Barbary macaques, and Ifrane to Fes and its guided medina. The last days pass Volubilis and Meknes to Rabat and Casablanca on the Atlantic, before a free day in Marrakech for the souks and last purchases.

                1. Day 1 · Welcome to Marrakech

                  We meet you at Marrakech airport and drive you to your hotel or riad. The rest of the day is free to rest or take a first walk towards the Jemaa el-Fna square. Night in Marrakech.

                  Overnight: MarrakechAirport welcome
                2. Day 2 · Marrakech with a local guide

                  A full day in the red city with a local guide. Walk the medina, its souks, palaces and hidden squares, have lunch in the heart of the old town, then finish in the calm of the Majorelle Garden. Night in Marrakech.

                  Overnight: MarrakechLocal guide · Majorelle Garden
                3. Day 3 · Marrakech to Ouarzazate

                  We cross the High Atlas by the Tizi n’Tichka pass, with stops for views and Berber villages. Visit the earthen kasbah of Ait Ben Haddou and the film studios of Ouarzazate, the ‘Hollywood of Africa’. Night in Ouarzazate.

                  Overnight: OuarzazateAit Ben Haddou · film studios
                4. Day 4 · Ouarzazate to Merzouga

                  Through Skoura and the Rose Valley to the Todra Gorge, where we walk between towering walls of rock. Then via Tinjdad and Erfoud to Merzouga, at the edge of the dunes of Erg Chebbi. Night in a hotel in Merzouga.

                  Overnight: hotel in MerzougaRose Valley · Todra Gorge
                5. Day 5 · Exploring the Merzouga area

                  A day to discover the desert around Erg Chebbi at a gentle pace: the dunes, the oases and the village of Khamlia, where the Gnawa families welcome you with their music. Sunset over the sand and a second night in your hotel in Merzouga.

                  Overnight: hotel in MerzougaKhamlia · Gnawa music
                6. Day 6 · Camel trek and night in the dunes

                  After lunch in Merzouga we visit a Berber family and a palm-fringed oasis, then ride camels with a local guide deep into Erg Chebbi, the highest dunes in Morocco, as the sun goes down. Traditional dinner, Berber drums around the fire and a night in a desert camp.

                  Overnight: desert campCamel trek · dinner at the camp
                7. Day 7 · Merzouga to Midelt

                  Sunrise over the dunes. We drive north through Rissani and its old market, Erfoud and the long palm groves of the Ziz Valley, past Errachidia to Midelt, at the foot of the Middle Atlas. Night in Midelt.

                  Overnight: MideltRissani · Ziz Valley
                8. Day 8 · Midelt to Fes via the cedar forest

                  The road climbs into the Middle Atlas to the great cedar forest of Azrou, where the Barbary macaques live among the trees. We continue through Ifrane, the ‘Switzerland of Morocco’, and arrive in Fes. Night in Fes.

                  Overnight: FesCedar forest · Ifrane
                9. Day 9 · Fes with a local guide

                  A full day in the medieval medina of Fes with a local guide: its lanes, souks and madrasas, the famous tanneries and the ceramic workshops. Night in a classic riad in Fes.

                  Overnight: riad in FesLocal guide · tanneries
                10. Day 10 · Fes to Rabat via Volubilis and Meknes

                  We leave Fes for Volubilis, the best-preserved Roman city in Morocco, then lunch in Meknes, the imperial city of monumental gates. In the afternoon we drive on to Rabat, the capital. Night in Rabat.

                  Overnight: RabatVolubilis · Meknes
                11. Day 11 · Rabat, Casablanca and Marrakech

                  Morning visit of Rabat: the Hassan Tower, the Mausoleum of Mohammed V and the Kasbah of the Udayas. On to Casablanca for a stop by the Atlantic and the Hassan II Mosque, with lunch or a drink at Rick’s Café if you wish, then the road back to Marrakech. Night in Marrakech.

                  Overnight: MarrakechRabat · Casablanca
                12. Day 12 · Free day and departure

                  Free time in Marrakech for last shopping in the souks and packing. At the agreed time we take you to Marrakech airport.
