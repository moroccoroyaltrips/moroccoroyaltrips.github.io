                5 Days from Agadir to Marrakech via the Sahara

                The complete southern crossing at a comfortable pace: saffron and kasbahs, the Dades and Todra gorges, a night in Erg Chebbi, the oases of the Saghro and the Draa, then Ait Ben Haddou and the High Atlas.

                5Days
                4Nights: Ouarzazate, Todra, desert camp, Agdz
                ~1,350Km from Agadir to Marrakech
                2Gorges: Dades and Todra
                Overview

                Five days to go from the Atlantic to Marrakech by way of the Sahara, with shorter driving days and time to enjoy each stop. It is the most comfortable way to cross the whole of southern Morocco from Agadir.

                The first day climbs through Taroudant and Taliouine, the land of saffron, to Ouarzazate, the gateway to the desert. The second follows the road of the thousand kasbahs through Skoura, the Rose Valley and the Dades to a night beside the Todra Gorge.

                On the third day the palm groves of Tinjdad lead to Erfoud and Rissani, then to Merzouga, where a camel carries you into the dunes of Erg Chebbi for sunset and a night at a desert camp. The fourth day returns west along Jbel Saghro, through Alnif and the oasis of Nkob, to the Draa Valley and Agdz.

                The last day reaches Ouarzazate and the kasbah of Ait Ben Haddou, a UNESCO World Heritage site, before the Tizi n’Tichka pass carries you over the High Atlas to Marrakech.

                Highlights
                • Taroudant & Taliouine saffron
                • Rose Valley & Dades
                • Todra Gorge
                • Camel trek in Erg Chebbi
                • Nkob oasis
                • Draa Valley
                • Ait Ben Haddou & Tizi n’Tichka
                1. Day 1 · Agadir to Ouarzazate

                  Morning pick-up in Agadir and east through the argan groves of the Souss plain to Taroudant, with a short stop by its ochre ramparts or in its souks. Up into the Anti-Atlas to Taliouine, the capital of Moroccan saffron, then across the high plateaus to Ouarzazate, the gateway to the desert, known for its kasbahs and film studios. Time to visit the Taourirt kasbah or simply relax. Dinner and night in a hotel or riad in Ouarzazate.

                  Overnight: hotel or riad in OuarzazateMeals: dinner
                2. Day 2 · Ouarzazate to the Todra Gorge

                  Morning pick-up in Ouarzazate and east to Skoura, an oasis of palm groves and old earthen kasbahs. Through Kelaat M’Gouna and the Rose Valley, known for its rosewater and local rose products, then into the Dades Valley and its strange rock formations, with stops for photos. On to Tinghir and the Todra Gorge, where you walk between rock walls hundreds of metres high. Dinner and night in a hotel or riad near the gorge.

                  Overnight: hotel or riad near TodraMeals: breakfast, dinner
                3. Day 3 · Todra to Merzouga

                  After breakfast, east through Tinjdad and the desert plains to Erfoud, the town of fossils, where you can visit a workshop that cuts and polishes them. In Rissani, the old caravan town, a stop at the market if it is a market day. Arrival in Merzouga in the afternoon and a camel trek into the dunes of Erg Chebbi for the sunset. Dinner at the camp and a night under the stars.

                  Overnight: desert campMeals: breakfast, dinner
                4. Day 4 · Merzouga to Agdz

                  Sunrise over the dunes and breakfast at the camp. Through Rissani and its traditional market, then west along the foot of Jbel Saghro, past the fossil town of Alnif and Tazarine, to the oasis of Nkob and its dozens of earthen kasbahs. On to Tansikht and the palm groves of the Draa Valley, following the valley road to Agdz, a quiet town at the foot of Jbel Kissane. Dinner and night in a hotel, riad or kasbah guesthouse in Agdz or nearby.

                  Overnight: hotel or riad in AgdzMeals: breakfast, dinner
                5. Day 5 · Agdz to Marrakech

                  After breakfast, over the Tizi n’Tinififft pass to Ouarzazate and on to the ksar of Ait Ben Haddou, a UNESCO World Heritage site and the finest example of earthen architecture in the south, with time to walk up its lanes. Then the High Atlas by the Tizi n’Tichka pass, through Berber villages, with stops for views and coffee. Arrival in Marrakech in the late afternoon or evening and drop-off at your accommodation.

                  Drop-off: MarrakechMeals: breakfast
